# Necropathy 0.7.0: play anywhere, fight together

![The cast of Necropathy over the moonlit graveyard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Smoketsu/Vampire-Survivors-Clone-Lol/v0.7.0/docs/media/v0.7.0/cover.png)

0.6.0 rebuilt the menus and the boss fights. **0.7.0 puts Necropathy in your browser and on your phone**. It adds:

- **nine new army synergies** for the newer summons;
- a new Covenant soldier, **the Sister of the Scourge**;
- a fresh look for the game's heroines;
- pixel art for the last placeholder objects in a run.

Your save carries over as always.

## Download and play

| Platform | File | How to run |
|---|---|---|
| Browser (desktop or phone) | Play on the game's itch.io page, or `Necropathy-web.zip` | Open the page and click to start. On a phone, hold it sideways. |
| Windows | `Necropathy-windows.zip` | Unzip and run `Necropathy.exe`. The build is unsigned, so SmartScreen may warn you: click **More info → Run anyway**. |
| Linux | `Necropathy-linux.zip` | Unzip, then `chmod +x Necropathy.x86_64` and run it. |

Keyboard and mouse, a controller, or touch all work. Every key can be rebound in **Settings**.

## Play in your browser

![A run in the browser](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Smoketsu/Vampire-Survivors-Clone-Lol/v0.7.0/docs/media/v0.7.0/browser.jpg)

- **The whole game runs in a desktop browser.** Nothing to install, about a 16 MB download.
- **The page never freezes while it starts.** The sounds and music are built a few milliseconds at a time.
- **Saves live in your browser** and survive a reload. They're separate from the desktop version's.
- **Music starts on your first click or key press**, because browsers require it.
- **Quit is hidden**: close the tab instead.

## Play on your phone

![Touch controls on a phone](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Smoketsu/Vampire-Survivors-Clone-Lol/v0.7.0/docs/media/v0.7.0/phone.jpg)

Hold your phone or tablet sideways and play with your thumbs.

- **Move** with a joystick that appears wherever your left thumb lands.
- **Buttons on the right:** a big **Grave Step** dash with its charges, **Dread Rally** (it glows when ready), the Shadow Bolt **aim** toggle, and a four-way **stance** cluster. **Pause** sits in the top-right corner.
- **Every menu works by tapping:**
  - Level-up cards, chests and sliders.
  - The Codex, which you drag to scroll.
  - The Ossuary: one tap shows an upgrade's details, a second buys it.
- **Text and buttons grow on small screens**, and tall screens shrink to fit.
- **Turn your phone upright and the game pauses** with a "Turn your device" screen.
- **New Effects setting (Full / Reduced):** phones start on Reduced, with fewer particles, only big damage numbers and less screen shake. It's purely visual.

## New synergies for the newer summons

![Synergies firing on the Crimson March](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Smoketsu/Vampire-Survivors-Clone-Lol/v0.7.0/docs/media/v0.7.0/synergies_in_battle.jpg)

Abominations, Rogues, Gargoyles, Frost Wraiths, Bone Hounds, Banshees and Corpse Catapults now combine like the original four. One class **marks** your foes, and another **cashes the mark in** for extra damage. Each newer class is part of two synergies:

| Synergy | Pair | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Flesh Rampart** | Abomination + Warriors | Stomped foes are staggered; Warriors and Death Knights hit them harder |
| **Killing Field** | Abomination + Corpse Catapult | Boulders hit staggered foes harder, and catapults aim at the knot an Abomination holds |
| **Death from Above** | Gargoyle + Rogues | Rogues hunt foes a Gargoyle just swooped on, hit them harder, and their dash resets on the first hit |
| **The Wild Hunt** | Gargoyle + Bone Hounds | A swooped foe becomes the pack's prey and takes harder bites |
| **Cold Blood** | Frost Wraith + Rogues | Rogues hit chilled and frozen foes much harder, and dash to them first |
| **Shatterfall** | Frost Wraith + Corpse Catapult | Boulders crush frozen foes, and catapults aim at frozen clusters |
| **Requiem** | Banshee + Mages | Mage blasts hit weakened foes harder and stretch the Dirge |
| **Baying of the Damned** | Banshee + Bone Hounds | Each Dirge sends the pack into a short frenzy |
| **The Charnel Menagerie** | Any 4 of the 7 newer classes | They deal more damage and return faster when they fall |

Like the original four, a synergy wakes when both classes reach level 2, and the level-up card tells you so. Each hit takes only its biggest synergy bonus, and the bonus is halved against the stage boss. Evolved forms count too. All 13 synergies now have their own pixel icons.

## A new foe: the Sister of the Scourge

![The Sister of the Scourge beside a Templar, the Wither Witch and a Halberdier](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Smoketsu/Vampire-Survivors-Clone-Lol/v0.7.0/docs/media/v0.7.0/sister.png)

The Covenant sends its battle-nuns into the war.

- **Her lash:** she stops, a crimson line flashes toward her target, and her scourge cracks along it, striking the first two bodies in its path. Step out of the line.
- **Zeal:** when a fellow soldier falls nearby, she surges forward faster for a moment.
- **Where she appears:**
  - Crimson March: from 6:00, with squads at 7:35 and 12:25. The second squad is led by an elite **Mother Superior**.
  - Sanctum of Judgment: from 7:45, with squads at 9:40 and 15:00.
- **Codex:** she has her own entry.

## No more placeholders

![The empowered graves on each stage](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Smoketsu/Vampire-Survivors-Clone-Lol/v0.7.0/docs/media/v0.7.0/graves.jpg)

Every object you meet in a run is now pixel art:

- **Empowered graves:** an open grave pouring soul-light on the Graveyard, a heap of fallen crusaders on the Crimson March, and cracking marble over a crypt in the Sanctum.
- **Graveyard events:** the Crypt Defender's crypt door, and the graves the Chaplains try to consecrate.
- **Combat markers:** the boss rally pennant, the ice shell on frozen foes, stun stars, the Death Mark skull, the Mage mark, the buff chevron, the Sun Paladin's ward and Seraphine's safe-spot crosses. Markers now float above heads instead of sitting on them.
- **Spell and summon props:** Corpse Explosion corpses, Bone Wall segments that rise out of the ground, Catapult skull-boulders (some burning), the Bone Oracle's shards, Eclipse Ward orbs and the Cathedral Horror's stone form.
- **Icons:** the last-resort level-up cards (Dark Restoration, Grave Fortitude, Call of the Grave) and the Ossuary's Oath tree.

## Fixes

- **The browser game no longer resets on phones after a while.** The engine's Web audio copied a sound's whole recording every time it played, and phones ran out of memory and reloaded the page. Every sound now reuses its one copy.
- **Sound on iPhone.** Safari muted the game whenever the phone was on silent, and could leave the sound paused after a call or a trip to another app. The game now plays like a video does, so you hear it in silent mode (other apps' music pauses while you play).
- **Fullscreen on iPhone.** iPhone Safari can't put a web page in fullscreen, so the Fullscreen toggle did nothing. On iPhone, Settings now shows what works instead: **Share → Add to Home Screen** opens the game fullscreen.
- **Saves on iPhone, and save codes.** iPhone Safari forgets the saves of a game embedded in another site (like the itch.io page) as soon as Safari closes. A new **Settings → Save** tab backs your progress up as a code: copy it, then paste it into **Restore** to bring your progress back, here or on another device. The game also gets its own page, whose saves iPhone Safari keeps.
- A summon could get wedged between a tree and a Covenant watchtower for the rest of a run. A summon that can't make its way back to you is now called back to your side.

## Balance

No numbers changed outside the new content. In a 96-game check across all three stages, a strong army build still kills each boss in about 1:10–1:30.
