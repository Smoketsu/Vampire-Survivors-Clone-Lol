# Necropathy v0.5.0 — the game has a name, a face and an ending

![The undead horde holds the line against Grand Inquisitor Seraphine](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Smoketsu/vampire-survivors-clone-lol/v0.5.0/docs/media/v0.5.0/hero.gif)

**Gravebound is now Necropathy.** This is the biggest update yet, with more than 150 changes since v0.4. Every unit, boss, spell and
menu has been redrawn as true pixel art. The campaign now has its final stage and final boss. There are twelve
necromancers to play, and the spell, summon and evolution rosters are much larger. **Your Gravebound save carries over
automatically**: Soul Ash, unlocks and settings are all kept.

## Download and play

| Platform | File | How to run |
|---|---|---|
| Windows | `Necropathy-windows.zip` | Unzip and run `Necropathy.exe`. The build is unsigned, so SmartScreen may warn you: click **More info → Run anyway**. |
| Linux | `Necropathy-linux.zip` | Unzip, then `chmod +x Necropathy.x86_64` and run it. |

Keyboard and mouse or a controller both work. Every key can be rebound in **Settings**.

## Stage 3: the Sanctum of Judgment

![The Sanctified Halls](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Smoketsu/vampire-survivors-clone-lol/v0.5.0/docs/media/v0.5.0/sanctum.jpg)

Slay Marshal Vael to open the last stage of the campaign: the Covenant's own cathedral.

- **Four battle zones**, sanctified chambers that weaken your undead, and three **sacred altars** you can destroy to
  weaken every soldier.
- **New Covenant soldiers.** The Sanctified Sentinel (armoured, with a telegraphed sweep), the Radiant Inquisitor
  (holy zones that suppress your army), the High Confessor (heals, holy shields and *revives the dead*: kill them
  first) and the Sun Paladin.
- **The Cathedral Guardian**, a miniboss with slams and a sweeping beam.
- **Grand Inquisitor Seraphine**, the final boss. She fights in three phases, with teleports, beams, a safe-zone nova
  and a collapsing arena, and she ascends into her Avatar form.

![Grand Inquisitor Seraphine](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Smoketsu/vampire-survivors-clone-lol/v0.5.0/docs/media/v0.5.0/seraphine.jpg)

## Twelve necromancers

Each spell now has its own necromancer. Each one starts with that spell, has one innate passive and a few stat
tweaks. **The Gravecaller** is the classic necromancer. To unlock any other, reach **level 5 with their spell** in any
run: the Bone Oracle, the Doomsayer, the Barrow Warden, the Soulbinder, the Wither Witch, the Grave Herald and more.

## A bigger grimoire and a bigger army

- **5 new spells** (12 in all), each with an evolution: **Death Mark** → Doom Brand, **Bone Wall** → Charnel Bastion,
  **Soul Chains** → Chains of the Damned, **Wither** → Necrotic Blight, **Raise Dead** → Legion of Bones.
- **3 new summon classes** (12 in all): pack-hunting **Bone Hounds**, the wailing **Banshee** and the siege
  **Corpse Catapult**.
- **8 new summon evolutions**: Winter Revenant, Shadow Reaper, Cathedral Horror, Gravebow, Bone Colossus, Hellhound,
  Keening Lady and the Ossuary Engine.
- **10 new passives**: Bone Plating, Unholy Precision, Unholy Resilience, Bound Souls, Grim Ledger, Spirit Binding,
  Restless Dead, Blood Scent, Mournsong and Siegecraft.
- **Evolutions are meant to be discovered.** Every evolution, spell or summon, needs its base at Lv 5 *and* its
  paired passive at Lv 5, and it comes from a treasure chest. Once you find a recipe, it appears in the new
  **Recipes** book in the pause menu and as an icon row on the level-up cards.

![Level-up cards](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Smoketsu/vampire-survivors-clone-lol/v0.5.0/docs/media/v0.5.0/levelup.jpg)

## Command your horde

- **Grave Step** (Shift / RB) is a shadow dash with a moment of invulnerability.
- **Dread Rally** (Space / LB) is an ultimate charged by kills. It heals you and sends your army into a surge.
- **Army stances** (keys 1-4 / right-stick flicks):
  - **Escort** (the classic formation);
  - **Onslaught** (the army surges at the enemy);
  - **Spearhead** (a wedge pointed where you move; unlocked in the Ossuary);
  - **Bone Ring** (a closed circle of blockers around you).

![Sir Alaric meets the horde](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Smoketsu/vampire-survivors-clone-lol/v0.5.0/docs/media/v0.5.0/alaric.jpg)

## Challenge and style

- **Hyper mode.** Beat Seraphine once to unlock it. Enemies scale twice as fast and bosses are tougher, for double
  the Soul Ash. Each Hyper clear earns a gold badge for that stage and character.
- **Skins** are purely cosmetic. Each character has a Hyper skin, and four more skins are earned through
  achievements: the Dread Sovereign, the Ivory Seer, the Plague Doctor and the Heretic Saint.

![Choose your necromancer, difficulty and modifiers](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Smoketsu/vampire-survivors-clone-lol/v0.5.0/docs/media/v0.5.0/prerun.jpg)

## A whole new look

- **True pixel art everywhere.** That covers every summon, soldier, boss, structure, pickup, projectile, obstacle,
  zone floor and icon, drawn crisp at 1 art pixel = 1 screen pixel.
- **Walk cycles** for 49 units, **attack poses** for the three bosses (Alaric's hammer, Vael's shieldwall,
  Seraphine's cast), and pixel-art **effect animations** for blasts, novas, hits and level-ups.
- **A pixel UI kit** with cut-corner frames, the new **NECROPATHY** logo and pixel fonts (Jersey 10 and Jacquard 24).
- **Card text** is down to one clear sentence per spell, summon and passive.

![Marshal Vael plants the Holy Standard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Smoketsu/vampire-survivors-clone-lol/v0.5.0/docs/media/v0.5.0/vael.jpg)

## Also in this release
