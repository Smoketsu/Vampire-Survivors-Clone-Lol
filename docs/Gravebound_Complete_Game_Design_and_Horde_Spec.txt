PART I — GAME DESIGN DOCUMENT (GDD v0.1)

1. HIGH CONCEPT

Title: GRAVEBOUND
Genre: Dark fantasy, single-player action roguelite / bullet heaven / horde survival
Perspective: Top-down 2D or 2.5D
Platform: PC first (Steam)
Run length: 20–30 minutes per level
Inspirations: Vampire Survivors, Brotato, Army of Ruin, Warcraft III undead faction

The player is a necromancer fighting an advancing fanatical holy order called
the Crimson Covenant, inspired aesthetically by the Scarlet Crusade from World
of Warcraft, but designed as an original faction. The player begins alone and
hunted, gaining shadow spells and an increasingly powerful autonomous undead
army. The campaign reverses the power dynamic: defend a graveyard, besiege
the crusaders' compound, then invade the interior and defeat their leader.

Three campaign stages:
1. The Desecrated Graveyard — Defend against the Covenant invasion.
2. The Crimson March — Advance with the undead against the outer compound.
3. The Sanctum of Judgment — Invade the compound for the final boss.

Core fantasy: Begin as the hunted. Raise the fallen. Become the army.
Extinguish the crusade.

2. DESIGN PILLARS

- THE ARMY IS YOUR WEAPON: Warriors, archers, mages and priests play distinct
  frontline, ranged, area-damage and support roles. Summons are the feature,
  not merely cosmetic projectiles.
- UNHOLY POWER VERSUS DIVINE ZEAL: The enemy uses holy magic, military
  formations, healing, protective wards and specialized undead hunters.
- BUILD DIVERSITY: Mass skeletons, elite undead, destructive shadow magic,
  sustain/defensive builds and hybrids should all be viable.
- ESCALATING SPECTACLE: The game evolves visibly from a fragile caster into
  an army leader amid large-scale magical conflict.

3. CORE GAMEPLAY LOOP

1. Choose level, skin, difficulty and modifiers at the main menu.
2. Begin run with a single starting skill.
3. Move to survive as enemies approach continuously.
4. Automatically cast equipped shadow spells and deploy summons.
5. Enemies drop XP crystals and sometimes other resources.
6. Level up and choose one of three randomized upgrades.
7. New enemies, elites, events and environmental threats arrive by time.
8. Build synergies, evolve abilities and replenish fallen summons.
9. Defeat final stage boss to unlock next chapter, currency and cosmetics.
10. Spend earned Soul Ash on permanent upgrades, then replay.

4. CONTROLS

WASD: Move
Mouse: Menu navigation
Shift: Optional unlocked dash
Space: Optional ultimate
Esc: Pause/settings
Tab: Expand army information
Controller left stick: Move
Controller A / Cross: Confirm

Combat, spells, summons, targeting and healing are automatic. No manual unit
selection, unit positioning or aiming is required.

5. PLAYER STATISTICS

Max Health — Player survivability
Movement Speed — Movement across battlefield
Armor — Physical damage mitigation
Shadow Resistance — Hostile magic damage mitigation
Spell Power — Shadow ability damage
Minion Damage — Summon damage
Minion Health — Summon survivability
Summon Capacity — Max active undead
Cooldown Reduction — Ability intervals
Area of Effect — Spell radii
Duration — Temporary effect duration
Pickup Radius — Experience collection distance
Luck — Reward probability

6. ABILITY SLOTS

6 active abilities (automatic summons and spells), 6 passive abilities and
1 optional ultimate. If active slots fill up, offer level-ups to existing
skills and passives. Skills have five normal levels; evolution requires a
matching passive and special elite reward.

7. FOUR PRIMARY UNDEAD SUMMONS

SKELETON WARRIOR — Frontline
- Basic rusted-sword melee attacker; engages enemies threatening player.
- Moderate HP, modest damage, limited body-block effect.
- Upgrades: extra population, more HP/damage, shields and armor, cleave.
- Evolution: Dreadguard Legion — armored skeleton fighters with retaliatory
  strikes and cleaving attacks.

SKELETAL ARCHER — Ranged sustained DPS
- Keeps distance, fires physical arrows, fragile.
- Upgrades: increased count, faster attack, piercing arrows, cursed arrows.
- Evolution: Gravebow Company — splitting/piercing arrows and curses.

UNDEAD MAGE — Magical AoE
- Targets clustered enemies with shadow projectiles or explosions.
- Upgrades: more mages, damage, splash, Soulburn, larger AoE.
- Evolution: Lich Conclave — chain explosions and shadow storms.

UNDEAD PRIEST — Healing and support
- Corrupted former cleric; heals allied undead, occasionally shields player,
  casts minor unholy offense when no healing is needed.
- Upgrades: more priests, healing amount, radius, defense buffs.
- Evolution: Profane Bishop — area healing aura and unholy damage pulse.

Possible future summons: Bone Golem (tank), Banshee (debuffer), Death Knight
(elite melee/buff commander), Corpse Abomination (durable monster).

8. SHADOW SPELL ABILITIES

SHADOW BOLT
- Automatically fires at nearby enemy at intervals.
- Upgrades: damage, extra projectiles, speed and pierce, damage-over-time
  curse. Evolution: Void Barrage — rapid piercing bolts, split on impact.

SHADOW ORB SHIELD
- Protective orbs orbit the necromancer, damage approaching enemies and
  intercept a limited number of hostile projectiles.
- Upgrades: orb count, orbit speed, radius, damage, interception.
- Evolution: Eclipse Ward — rotating barrier and periodic damage pulses.
- Interception is charge- or cooldown-limited to avoid invulnerability.

SHADOW BLAST
- Automatically creates AoE explosion around the player.
- Upgrades: damage, radius, cooldown, slow.
- Evolution: Abyssal Detonation — double-hit blast and short enemy pull.

Other possible spells:
- Grasp of the Damned: Hands from ground slow/root enemies.
- Soul Siphon: Nearby damage and capped lifesteal.
- Death Nova: Wave dealing bonus damage to cursed enemies.
- Grave Eruption: Bone spikes create damaging ground zones.

9. EVOLUTIONS AND PASSIVES

Skeleton Warrior + Bone Plating = Dreadguard Legion
Skeletal Archer + Unholy Precision = Gravebow Company
Undead Mage + Forbidden Knowledge = Lich Conclave
Undead Priest + Dark Benediction = Profane Bishop
Shadow Bolt + Soul Conduit = Void Barrage
Shadow Orb Shield + Warding Ritual = Eclipse Ward
Shadow Blast + Abyssal Focus = Abyssal Detonation

Army synergies:
- Shieldwall of the Damned (Warriors + Priests): Priest barriers on frontline.
- Volley of the Fallen (Archers + Mages): Mage marks cause archer bonus damage.
- March of the Deathless (3 different summons): Move speed + regeneration.
- The Unholy Host (all 4 main summons): Army-wide attack speed/resilience
  aura and visual effect on summon.

10. ENEMY FACTION — CRIMSON COVENANT

A fanatical, original religious-military order dedicated to eradicating
necromancy. Scarlet Crusade-inspired aesthetic: crimson and ivory tabards,
polished silver armor, cloaks and religious insignia. Their magic appears
radiant gold/white, contrasting player purple/green necromancy.

Roster:
- Covenant Recruit: Weak basic infantry, mass waves.
- Crimson Footman: Armored melee, moderate damage resistance.
- Zealous Archer: Ranged projectiles.
- Covenant Chaplain: Heals enemy allies.
- Purifier: Bonus damage versus undead summons.
- Templar Knight: Heavy charging melee; knockback/disruption.
- Radiant Inquisitor: Elite caster creating holy damage zones.
- Banner Bearer: Buffs nearby Covenant soldiers.
- Sanctified Sentinel: High-armor construct with sweeps.
- High Confessor: Elite priest healing, shielding and limited revival.

Faction tactics:
- Infantry protect archers and priests in advancing formations.
- Holy healers demand target prioritization.
- Purifiers attack the horde to exploit its reliance on summons.
- Banners buff formations until destroyed.
- Holy suppression/consecration zones apply localized temporary pressure.

20. DESIGN RISKS AND SUCCESS CRITERIA

Risks:
- Army overcrowding -> collision rules, separation steering, opacity options.
- Too-passive combat -> telegraphed hazards, positioning-sensitive threats.
- Excessive anti-undead suppression -> localized, telegraphed counterplay.
- Mandatory builds -> test pure summoner, pure magic and hybrid clears.
- Overscope -> polish graveyard vertical slice before later content.

Success means unique and recognizable summon roles, meaningful level-up
choices, engaging holy enemy counters, distinctive stages, viable build types,
readable large battles, and strong replay value.

PART II — AUTONOMOUS HORDE TECHNICAL DESIGN (v0.2)

21. CORE OBJECTIVE AND BOUNDARIES

The player ONLY moves the necromancer and selects run upgrades. Every undead
unit spawns, follows, targets, attacks, heals, retreats and regroups without
manual commands. The system must feel coordinated rather than perfectly
omniscient, and be predictable enough for player positioning to matter.

Excluded from current scope: RTS selection, unit click-to-move, manual focus
fire, unit inventories, persistent named companions, tactical pause orders.

22. HORDE COMPONENT RESPONSIBILITIES

Player / AbilityManager: Current skills and effective stat modifiers.
HordeDirector: Population limits, replenishment scheduling, formation anchor
assignment, threat context, registered summons, buffs and target reservations.
SummonedUnit: Its own movement, target choice, attack/heal, animation and HP.
SpatialIndex: Shared nearby-enemy/ally lookup.
CombatResolver: Hits, armor, death, status effects and healing.
ProjectileManager: Projectiles, pooling and impacts.
EnemyDirector: Enemy waves, enemy density and event escalation.

23. BASE UNIT DATA (INITIAL PROTOTYPE ONLY)

                     Warrior   Archer   Mage   Priest
HP                      60       30       35      40
Attack damage           10        8       14       3
Attack interval        1.2s     1.6s     2.5s     2.0s
Preferred range         22px    170px    145px    150px
Movement speed          85       95       82      90
Level 1 count            2        1        1       1
Unit radius             10px      9px     10px     10px

For priest, offensive attack is used only when no heal is needed. Example
healing throughput in balance sandbox was 8 HP per cast at 2.0s interval,
subject to tuning and skill level. Coordinate baseline ~32px per world tile.

Army cap = sum of equipped summon abilities' class caps + global bonuses.
Level-one composition with all four abilities = 5 total summons.
Target common late-game composition = 20–40 undead.

24. SUMMON LIFECYCLE

SPAWN -> ORIENT -> TARGET -> ENGAGE/REPOSITION -> REPEAT -> DIE/DESPAWN

Create a summon when skill is learned, capacity increases, timer replaces a
casualty, or a temporary summon effect activates.

Spawn location: Ground point ~35–80px from player, navigable, not solid
world geometry, preferably not inside hazards or enemy collider. Failed
spawns retry rather than consume capacity/cooldown.

Initial sequential per-ability replacement intervals:
Warrior 5s; Archer 8s; Mage 12s; Priest 15s.
If an ability can hold 5 warriors and only 3 survive, spawn the missing 2
one at a time per interval. Each parent ability controls its own queue.

On death: Stop AI/attacks, deregister from targetable sets, release target
and anchor reservations, play death FX, pool unit and queue replacement.
Corpses are cosmetic and non-collidable.

Leash: Engagement zone ~220px from player; soft recall at 320px; hard
spectral fade/teleport at 650px for stranded units. Hard recall should be rare.

25. FINITE STATE MACHINE

States: SPAWNING, FOLLOWING, ACQUIRING, ENGAGING, REPOSITIONING,
CASTING, RETREATING, RETURNING, DEAD.

Transitions:
SPAWNING -> FOLLOWING when emergence finishes.
FOLLOWING -> ACQUIRING when a viable enemy is found.
ACQUIRING -> ENGAGING when target chosen; otherwise FOLLOWING.
ENGAGING -> CASTING/attack when in valid range and ready.
ENGAGING -> REPOSITIONING if line of sight or range inadequate.
ENGAGING -> RETURNING when target pursuit exceeds leash.
ENGAGING -> ACQUIRING when target dies.
REPOSITIONING -> ENGAGING when firing position achieved.
RETURNING -> FOLLOWING after re-entering army region.
Any alive state -> DEAD at zero HP.

Suggested rates: movement physics 60Hz; general AI decisions 5Hz;
target checks 3–5Hz; formation anchors 4Hz; player threat checks 5Hz.
Stagger AI work among unit groups to avoid synchronized spikes.

26. SOFT FORMATION / POSITIONING

Use non-rigid circles around player, weighted toward threats.
Inner defense band 35–85px: Warriors.
Midline 85–155px: Archers and mages.
Support band 60–125px: Priests.
These are preferences; role-based behavior overrides anchors when warranted.

Assign player-relative angular slots spread across rings. Add slight stable
random offsets to avoid perfect geometry. Threat direction biases warriors
toward approaching enemies; support avoids dangerous crowd densities.

Steering vector combines formation attraction, target pursuit, ally
separation, obstacle avoidance, and threat avoidance; weighted and clamped.

Example weights:
Warrior: pursuit 1.0, formation 0.35, ally separation 0.8,
         obstacle avoidance 1.2, threat avoidance 0.1.
Priest:  pursuit 0.0 while healing, formation 0.7, separation 0.8,
         obstacle avoidance 1.2, threat avoidance 1.0.
These are tuneable data values, not strict physics constants.

Collision design:
- Player <-> friendly undead: NO physical collision.
- Friendly <-> friendly: NO hard collisions; steering separation only.
- Friendly <-> enemy: No blocking collision for initial prototype; enemies
  can still hit allied summons via attacks and overlapping hit detection.
- Units <-> solid world: YES collision.
- Corpses: NO physics collision.

Open arenas use steering. Indoor halls can use navigation for major routes,
local avoidance for small obstacles and recall if pathing fails. Never
recalculate full paths every physics frame.

27. TARGET ACQUISITION

Unit queries shared spatial index for living, targetable candidate enemies
within its acquisition range; ranged attacks may require line of sight.

Score target (normalize all constituent measures):
score = distance_value*Wd + threat_to_player*Wt + role_priority*Wr
      + tactical_value*Wv - overkill_penalty

Warriors prefer enemies attacking player, close-range intruders, accessible
ranged casters, then nearest available enemy. Don't chase distant targets
while necromancer is surrounded.

Archers prefer exposed chaplains/healers, enemy archers/casters,
near-death finishable enemies, then unblocked nearby targets.

Mages prefer largest valuable AoE cluster, groups with support/elite targets,
groups closing on player, then isolated units when needed. Score potential
impact positions, generally based on enemy centers and nearby units.

Priests prioritize critically injured necromancer IF their skill explicitly
allows player healing; allies below 35% HP; allies with large missing
normalized HP; large damaged clusters if AoE heal unlocked; minor offense
when nothing to heal.

Target commitment: Hold reasonable target for >=1s; instantly switch on
death/invalidity; early switch only for a significantly greater threat.
Overkill: HordeDirector tracks rough committed ranged damage; redirect new
shots when target is already expected to die. Approximate, not exact.

28. WARRIOR AI

Acquire close threat -> approach within ~22px -> melee swing -> hit on
animation impact -> cooldown -> repeat. Intercept enemies within ~80px of
player; don't stray excessively. Evolution Dreadguard: armor, cleave,
reactive strikes, larger silhouette.

29. ARCHER AI

Choose ranged-priority target -> move into ~170px preferred range -> check
line of sight -> fire arrow -> cooldown. If threatening enemy is within
~45px, kite/retreat if space permits; otherwise keep firing.
Evolution Gravebow: piercing, cursed arrows, volleys, longer acquisition.

30. MAGE AI

Collect nearby enemy candidates -> score circular explosion opportunities
based on counts, clustered high-value units and proximity to player -> cast
at best position -> projectile/impact -> splash and optional Soulburn ->
cooldown. Evolution Lich Conclave: larger blasts, chains, curses, periodic
shadow-storm AoE.

31. PRIEST AI

Collect damaged nearby allies -> heal target with highest weighted urgency
-> approach range -> cast -> apply capped heal -> cooldown -> seek next.
Proposed normalized healing score:
0.6*(missing health fraction) + 0.2*(normalized missing raw HP)
+ 0.2*(tactical role priority).
Warriors may get a small role bonus. Never heal full-HP allies. If player
healing is enabled, critical necromancer has top priority. Evolution Profane
Bishop adds group heal aura, defensive blessing and damage pulse.

32. SHARED ARMY COORDINATION

HordeDirector holds living unit registry, class caps, anchor ownership,
local player-threat summary, summon queues, buffs and rough pending ranged
damage reservations.

Threat categories: immediate melee, ranged cluster, holy support,
elite/boss, environmental hazards. Raise warrior defense priority during
player danger while keeping archers/mages providing damage and priests
healing. Once threats disappear, return to formation band preferences.

Bosses: Warriors engage but respect leash; archers prioritize boss unless
urgent support target appears; mages target boss+adds for useful splash;
priests sustain allied units. Bosses can suppress or kill squads with clear
telegraphs but never permanently disable summon abilities.

33. SHADOW MAGIC INTERACTIONS

Shadow Bolt can preferentially hit enemies threatening warriors; future
curse marks can increase minion damage.
Shadow Orb Shield protects player from threats slipping through frontline;
future upgrades may grant nearby minions defense.
Shadow Blast slows/clusters nearby foes and creates intercept opportunities;
future synergy can buff minion move speed or reduce replacement timers.

34. SUMMON SKILL PROGRESSION

L2: +1 warrior, +10% damage.
L3: +2 warriors, +15% HP.
L4: +1 warrior, +20% attack speed.
L5: +2 warriors, +20% HP.

Global passives:
- Grave Dominion: Capacity.
- Necrotic Vigor: Max minion health.
- Dark Command: Minion attack damage/speed.
- Soul Recycling: Faster replacement.
- Unholy Resilience: Armor/AoE resistance.
- Bound Souls: Speed and leash/recall improvements.

Current summons recalculate effective attributes upon upgrades. Health
percentage is preserved on max HP increase unless healing explicitly occurs.
Recommended stacking: Add like-category bonus percentages, then multiply
separate categories. Make formula consistent and player-visible.

35. CRIMSON COVENANT INTERACTION RULES

Purifiers prioritize undead and deal minion bonus damage; relatively
vulnerable to ranged focus.
Chaplains heal other enemies; increase ranged-unit target preference.
Templars charge and disrupt melee army, threatening player through gaps.
Banner Bearers buff near soldiers; elevated target priority.
Radiant Inquisitors produce holy hazards; later difficulty may briefly
suppress replacement locally with telegraphed visuals.
Boss suppression must allow normal replacement afterward.

38. OPTIMIZATION AND PERFORMANCE

UNIT:
function update_behavior():
    if health <= 0:
        enter_state(DEAD)
        return
    if distance_to_player > hard_recall:
        perform_safe_recall()
        return
    if distance_to_player > soft_recall:
        enter_state(RETURNING)
        return
    if state == CASTING:
        continue_cast()
        return
    if has_valid_target():
        if can_attack_target(): perform_attack_if_ready()
        else: reposition_for_attack()
        return
    target = acquire_best_target()
    if target != null: enter_state(ENGAGING)
    else: enter_state(FOLLOWING)

TARGET SELECTOR:
function acquire_best_target():
    candidates = spatial_index.get_nearby_enemies(position, acquisition_range)
    best = null
    best_score = -infinity
    for enemy in candidates:
        if not is_valid_target(enemy): continue
        score = evaluate_target(enemy, target_profile)
        if score > best_score:
            best = enemy
            best_score = score
    return best

REPLENISHMENT:
function update_summon_ability(delta):
    if active_count >= current_capacity:
        replenish_timer = 0
        return
    replenish_timer += delta
    if replenish_timer >= replenish_interval:
        if try_spawn_summon(): replenish_timer = 0

PRIEST TARGETING:
function choose_healing_target():
    allies = spatial_index.get_nearby_allies(position, healing_range)
    best = null
    score_best = 0
    for ally in allies:
        if ally.health >= ally.max_health: continue
        score = calculate_healing_priority(ally)
        if score > score_best:
            score_best = score
            best = ally
    return best
