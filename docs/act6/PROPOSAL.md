## Act VI: The Inferno
Hell comes to collect. Every soul the necromancer raised was owed to the Pit, and the demons fight for them the way the Eye fought for the dead in Act II, but louder, hotter and far more tempting. The look is obsidian, ash, lava orange and crimson-violet demon skin, with charm-pink magic as the act's signature colour.

### Shared rules
1. Hell Pits (your idea). Burning pools on the ground that damage anyone standing in them, demons excepted. Some are fixed, some open on a telegraph (a glowing rim for 1.5 s) and close again. Luring the living-fodder damned through them is the counterplay; the army's ring has to flow around them. One ground path, like the furnace channels of Act III.
2. Portals (your idea). Demon portals are structures that pour out a squad every few seconds. They can be destroyed by damage, or sealed: the necromancer standing inside a portal's ring for 3 s closes it at once and pays a Soul Ash bounty. It forces him to walk into danger, which no other act asks.
3. Temptation. Succubi and lilim charm summons: a charmed summon (pink hearts over it) fights for hell for 6 s, then comes back. One path, HordeDirector.charm(), built on Act II's Claims pattern, capped at 3 charmed at once. Counters: kill the charmer and its charms break; Faithless (the act's counter-passive) shortens them.

### How far the style goes (owner: "to the limit of how suggestive you can make it")
Act VI is the most suggestive the game gets, and it goes right up to the line. The most exaggerated hourglass figures in the game; sultry poses, looks and animations (a hip-swaying walk, the bust bounce on every heroine and succubus, blown kisses); costumes cut as close as they can go: lingerie-styled corsets and armour, bare backs, shoulders, midriffs and thighs, deep necklines, very high slits, stockings and thigh-high boots. The one line it does not cross: no nudity and nothing sexually explicit, so chest and hips stay covered. That line is not a matter of taste: the image API refuses anything past it, so that art could not be made, and the game is public on GitHub Pages with no age gate. The approved Act VI unit lineup under Concept art is the target for the Act VI sprites.

### Stage 16: The Burning Gate
A plain of ash and obsidian before the gate of hell, the river of fire Phlegethon along its edge.


Zones: the Ash Plain, the Cages, the River of Fire, the Gate.

Miniboss, the Gatekeeper, a chained giant that drags the damned in.

Boss, Moloch, the Furnace King: a colossal bull-headed demon with a furnace for a belly. He swallows summons (they come back burning, as his for 6 s), vents fire waves, and cracks the arena into pits.

### Stage 17: The Palace of Silk and Ash
The court of desire: velvet halls, mirrors, rose gardens of black thorns, a ballroom over a lava lake.


Zones: the Rose Garden, the Hall of Mirrors (reflections of the necromancer confuse summon targeting), the Ballroom, the Queen's Chamber.

Miniboss, the Twin Succubi: two sisters who charm in turns; kill one and the other rages.

Boss, Lilith, Queen of the Night, Mother of Demons (the style at 11): charm waves that turn a whole arc of the ring for a few seconds, a kiss that marks the necromancer (while marked, every heal on him is turned into damage for 5 s), demon children born from her wings, and a final phase where she takes her true demon form.

### Stage 18: Pandemonium
The capital of hell and the campaign's last stage: an obsidian cathedral city, chains, rivers of lava, and the throne.


Zones: the Iron City, the Chain Bridges, the Cathedral of Ash, the Throne.

Miniboss, the Fallen Seraph General.

Boss, the Morning Star (Lucifer / the Adversary). Three phases: the Angel (beautiful, six burnt wings, a burning sword, light and dark beams), the Beast (the throne-skull form: huge, slamming, the whole arena pits), the Throne (the Adversary sits and makes the arena itself fight: every rule of Acts I–VI returns for one wave each, a boss rush of mechanics). When he falls, the necromancer takes the throne: the dead of heaven, earth and hell answer to him.

### Rewards
| Boss | Spell (paired passive) | Its character |
|---|---|---|
| Moloch | Brimstone: burning stones fall on the densest group and leave a short pit → Rain of Fire (+ Furnace Heart) | The Furnace Priest |
| Lilith | Temptation: charm the strongest nearby enemy to fight for you for 6 s → Unholy Desire (+ Velvet Chains) | The Succubus Queen (figure style at 11) |
| The Morning Star | Fall from Grace: a fallen star crashes on the strongest enemy → Lightbringer (+ Pride) | The Fallen Saint (figure style) |


Summon classes: the Succubus (unlocked by Lilith, like the Fallen Seraph by Seraphine: a flying charm support; evolution Lilim Matriarch) and the finale's Archfiend (a winged heavy demon; evolution Prince of Hell).

Counter-passives (Act VI only): Salt Circle (summons take less pit damage), Faithless (charms on summons last half as long).

Achievements: Gate Crashed, Unmoved (beat Lilith without a single summon charmed), Morning Star Falls (beat the Adversary; opens the final epilogue and the end of the campaign).



## Concept art notes (from the proposal)

The lineup: imp, hell knight, succubus, hellhound, damned soul, demon brute, lilim witch, pit cultist. The succubus and the lilim show the proposed "up to 11" level.

The approved level (owner: "perfect"): all nine Act VI units, with the succubus, lilim witch and fallen angel at the suggestiveness and curves the Act VI sprites should match. It was made curvier, then dialled back about 10%; the other versions are kept as act6_units_v1 / _v2. Use the original models for Male units

Lilith and the Morning Star: the queen of the second stage and the final boss on his skull throne (his Beast form).

Stage 16 in play: hell pits, two portals pouring out imps, and Temptation (a succubus's kiss has turned one skeleton of the ring pink).
Notes from making them: the first Act IV and V lineups drew every soldier as a woman, because the figure-style instruction spread to all figures, so the sprite prompts must name each figure's sex (the all-female versions are kept as _v1 in case you like them). The first Act VI lineup drew two figures bare-chested and was thrown away; the version above is the second.
