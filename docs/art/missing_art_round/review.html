Missing Art Round

Everything the Nov 2026 missing-art audit asked for, drawn on the art grid (1 art px = 1 screen px) in the locked palette by art_source/missing_art/make_missing_art.py (deterministic, no image generation, so no API cost). Each item at 1x and 4x on a dark background. Effects (groups 1 and 4, the Holmgang ring) are not wired yet: see docs/art/missing_art_round/WIRING.md. Obstacles, markers, icons and decals sit at the keys the code already asks for. Derived pieces (the vertical obstacle variants) are marked.
