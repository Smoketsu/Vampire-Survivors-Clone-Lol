Concept art sheet for a top-down Vampire Survivors-style game, in the exact pixel-art style of the two reference sprites (a hooded necromancer and a skeleton warrior): true pixel art, chunky readable pixels, a dark 1 px outline, a small limited palette, flat shading with a few tones, 3/4 top-down view, figures shown full body. The references are for style and scale only; do not copy them unless the scene asks for the necromancer or skeletons. Every female figure has an exaggerated hourglass figure and is fully clothed (chest and hips always covered). Male figures are clearly male. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI.

A lineup of ancient Egyptian enemies on a plain dark blue-black night desert background, in a row: 1) a male mummy, wrapped and shambling; 2) a female mummy, wrapped, faster; 3) a scarab beetle swarm; 4) a male khopesh guard with a curved sword and a striped headcloth; 5) a female Nile archer with a bow, linen dress and gold collar; 6) a male Heka priest with a shaved head, leopard-skin robe and a glowing gold glyph; 7) a female embalmer with linen wraps and a jar; 8) a male jackal-headed warrior with a spear; 9) a female cat-masked Bastet hunter with twin daggers; 10) a male crocodile warden rising from sand; 11) a stone ushabti statue servant. Palette: dull bronze-gold, lapis blue, sandstone, carnelian red, papyrus green.
