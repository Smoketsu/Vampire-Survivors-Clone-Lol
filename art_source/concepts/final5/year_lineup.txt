Concept art sheet for a top-down Vampire Survivors-style game, in the exact pixel-art style of the two reference sprites (a hooded necromancer and a skeleton warrior): true pixel art, chunky readable pixels, a dark 1 px outline, a small limited palette, flat shading with a few tones, 3/4 top-down view, figures shown full body. The references are for style and scale only; do not copy them unless the scene asks for the necromancer or skeletons. Every female figure has an exaggerated hourglass figure and is fully clothed (chest and hips always covered). Male figures are clearly male. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI.

A lineup of nature enemies, the Wild, on a plain dark green background with falling leaves, in a row: 1) a swarm of small thornlings (twig creatures); 2) a female dryad with bark skin, leaf dress and a flower crown, planting a glowing seed; 3) a huge treant with root feet; 4) a male fae knight in leaf-green armour with a thorn sword; 5) a glowing will-o'-wisp; 6) a great stag with huge antlers; 7) a male rider of the Wild Hunt, a spectral antlered horseman with hounds; 8) a female spore mother, a mushroom-capped figure in an autumn gown releasing spores; 9) a female winter fae in a white and icy blue gown with frost wings; 10) a corpse-bloom, a giant carnivorous flower. Palette: moss green, bark brown, blossom pink, gold leaves, frost white.
