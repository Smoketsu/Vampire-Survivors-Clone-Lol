Concept art sheet for a top-down Vampire Survivors-style game, in the exact pixel-art style of the two reference sprites (a hooded necromancer and a skeleton warrior): true pixel art, chunky readable pixels, a dark 1 px outline, a small limited palette, flat shading with a few tones, 3/4 top-down view, figures shown full body. The references are for style and scale only; do not copy them unless the scene asks for the necromancer or skeletons. Every female figure has an exaggerated hourglass figure and is fully clothed (chest and hips always covered). Male figures are clearly male. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI.

A lineup of Norse enemies on a plain dark background with drifting snow, in a row: 1) a male raider with a round shield and an axe; 2) a female shield-maiden with a round shield, a spear and braided hair; 3) a male berserker in a bear pelt with red glowing eyes and two axes, frenzied; 4) a male skald with a horn and a lyre, singing; 5) a female valkyrie with white-gold wings, a winged helm and a spear, flying; 6) a male draugr, a blue-grey undead warrior in barrow armour with frost on him; 7) a huge male frost giant with a boulder; 8) a grey wolf of the wastes; 9) a female seer (volva) with a staff and a hood, a blue rune glowing in her hand; 10) a carved rune-stone with glowing blue-white runes. Palette: frost white, iron grey, pine green-black, hearth amber, valkyrie gold, ice-blue rune glow.
