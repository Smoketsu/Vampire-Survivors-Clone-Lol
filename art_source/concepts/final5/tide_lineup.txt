Concept art sheet for a top-down Vampire Survivors-style game, in the exact pixel-art style of the two reference sprites (a hooded necromancer and a skeleton warrior): true pixel art, chunky readable pixels, a dark 1 px outline, a small limited palette, flat shading with a few tones, 3/4 top-down view, figures shown full body. The references are for style and scale only; do not copy them unless the scene asks for the necromancer or skeletons. Every female figure has an exaggerated hourglass figure and is fully clothed (chest and hips always covered). Male figures are clearly male. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI.

A lineup of pirate enemies and sea creatures on a plain dark navy background, in a row: 1) a male deckhand with a cutlass and a striped shirt; 2) a male musketeer with a long musket and a tricorn; 3) a male powder runner sprinting with a lit powder keg; 4) a female swashbuckler with a rapier, long coat and boots; 5) a male grappler swinging a hook line; 6) a female gunnery mate holding a round grenado with a lit fuse; 7) a male boatswain with a whistle and a bellowing mouth; 8) a female voodoo navigator with a bone charm and a glowing amber sigil; 9) a huge male boarding bruiser with a boarding axe; then sea creatures: a reef shark breaching, a giant hull crab, a kraken tentacle rising from foam, a sea serpent coil. Palette: tarred oak, sailcloth cream, brass, powder-smoke grey, sash red, lantern amber, deep navy sea with white foam.
