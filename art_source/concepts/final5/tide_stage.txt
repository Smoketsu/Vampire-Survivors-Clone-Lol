Concept art sheet for a top-down Vampire Survivors-style game, in the exact pixel-art style of the two reference sprites (a hooded necromancer and a skeleton warrior): true pixel art, chunky readable pixels, a dark 1 px outline, a small limited palette, flat shading with a few tones, 3/4 top-down view, figures shown full body. The references are for style and scale only; do not copy them unless the scene asks for the necromancer or skeletons. Every female figure has an exaggerated hourglass figure and is fully clothed (chest and hips always covered). Male figures are clearly male. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI.

A gameplay mock-up seen from the top-down game camera: the deck of a small sloop at night on a dark sea, a single mast, a hatch and powder barrels along the rails, all fitting in the frame with water all around. In the centre the hooded necromancer from the reference stands inside a ring of skeleton warriors (the Bone Ring), the ring flattened to fit the narrow deck. Two small rowboats have hooked the rail with grapple ropes and pirates climb aboard; a dotted wake line and an amber lantern show where a third boat will arrive. A kraken tentacle rises at the rail and grabs a skeleton. White foam lines on the water. Small figures at game scale, readable.
