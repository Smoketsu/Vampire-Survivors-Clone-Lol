STAGE MOOD (top-down three-quarter view like the attached game, as a gameplay mock-up without UI): THE ASPHODEL UNDERWORLD, stage 11. A pale grey field of asphodel flowers under a starless dark sky, the black River Styx winding across with Charon's boat, broken Greek columns, and glowing orange-red FISSURES cracking open in the ground (Hades' hazard: one fissure telegraphed as a thin red line, one wide open spewing pale ghost shades). In the middle the violet-hooded necromancer inside a ring of his skeleton army (the Bone Ring) fights a phalanx of bronze hoplites whose round shields are locked together into a wall, and a bronze wolf pack running at the ring. Far at the top, on a black throne at the river's end, HADES watches: a gaunt god in a black chiton with a helm of darkness and a bident, Cerberus the three-headed hound at his feet. Colours: asphodel grey #6B6E6A #A9ABA3, Styx black #0B1214 #1B2B2E, fissure fire #FF8C26 #FFDC66, shade teal #7FB5AC, bronze #B07A33 #E3B866, necromancer violet.

STYLE: concept art for a dark 2D top-down pixel-art game (attached: the game's own sprites, the necromancer and his skeletons, for the pixel size, outline weight and look). True pixel art: crisp square pixels, every pixel a flat colour block about 4 image pixels wide, nearest-neighbour, a 1-pixel dark outline around every figure, light from the upper left, at most 3 shades per material, big bold readable shapes, faces that read clearly. No dithering, no gradients, no soft glow haze, no blur, no text, no letters, no labels, no watermark, no UI, no border. Fully opaque.
