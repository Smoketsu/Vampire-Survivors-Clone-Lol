STAGE MOOD (top-down three-quarter view like the attached game, as a gameplay mock-up without UI): THE CRASH SITE, stage 25. The ruins of a capital city: broken concrete, a huge crater with a smoking half-buried grey-white Harvester scout ship lying in it, a glass spire in the background. TERRAFORMING: patches of glowing green-and-purple alien ground (veined fleshy turf with spores) are spreading across the rubble, with alien-green healing glow on Brood units standing on it, and a few patches burnt back black by the necromancer's spell. BEAMS: two thin magenta aiming lines and one thick magenta laser beam cutting straight across the arena through bodies. ABDUCTION: a white-hulled flying saucer hovers above the ring with a green tractor beam lifting a skeleton into the air. Brood broodlings swarm in from the crater, a burrower erupts from a dirt mound, and a Lumen Sentinel shoots a beam. In the middle the violet-hooded necromancer inside a ring of his skeleton army. Colours: rubble grey #4A4C52 #6A6D75, hull white #E8ECF0, plasma green #3CFF6A #1FA84A, alien flesh purple #5A2A6A, laser magenta #FF2FB8, ground dark #1A1C22, necromancer violet.

STYLE: concept art for a dark 2D top-down pixel-art game (attached: the game's own sprites, the necromancer and his skeletons, for the pixel size, outline weight and look). True pixel art: crisp square pixels, every pixel a flat colour block about 4 image pixels wide, nearest-neighbour, a 1-pixel dark outline around every figure, light from the upper left, at most 3 shades per material, big bold readable shapes, faces that read clearly. No dithering, no gradients, no soft glow haze, no blur, no text, no letters, no labels, no watermark, no UI, no border. Fully opaque.
