Concept art sheet for a top-down Vampire Survivors-style game, in the exact pixel-art style of the two reference sprites (a hooded necromancer and a skeleton warrior): true pixel art, chunky readable pixels, a dark 1 px outline, a small limited palette, flat shading with a few tones, 3/4 top-down view, figures shown full body. The references are for style and scale only; do not copy them unless the scene asks for the necromancer or skeletons. Every female figure has an exaggerated hourglass figure and is fully clothed (chest and hips always covered). Male figures are clearly male. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI.

The three bosses of the Sanguine Court in one gothic scene under a huge blood-red full moon: on the left Vargr the Moon-Eater, a gigantic male werewolf king with a crown of bone and a silver-scarred chest, howling; in the middle Isolde Vane, the Witch of the Red Moon, a female witch in a flowing crimson and black gown with a deep neckline, floating over a bubbling cauldron with two faint ghostly copies of herself behind her (her three sisters); on the right Queen Carmilla, a female vampire queen in an elegant black and blood-red ballgown with bare shoulders, a high slit and a ruby crown, kneeling beside an open stone coffin from which the Elder rises: an ancient, gaunt male vampire horror with vast bat wings. Palette: blood red, black, candle gold, moonlit silver.
