Concept art sheet for a top-down Vampire Survivors-style game, in the exact pixel-art style of the two reference sprites (a hooded necromancer and a skeleton warrior): true pixel art, chunky readable pixels, a dark 1 px outline, a small limited palette, flat shading with a few tones, 3/4 top-down view, figures shown full body. The references are for style and scale only; do not copy them unless the scene asks for the necromancer or skeletons. Every female figure has an exaggerated hourglass figure and is fully clothed (chest and hips always covered). Male figures are clearly male. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI.

Five ORIGINAL superheroes (not resembling any existing comic book characters) posed together on a city rooftop at night: in the centre Paragon, the Last Hero, a tall powerful male hero in a white and gold suit with a long cape, floating, eyes glowing gold; around him Blitz, a lean male speedster in red with lightning streaks trailing behind; Atlas, a huge muscular male strongman in green lifting a wrecked car overhead; Aurora, a female flying energy blaster in a sleek violet and teal suit, hands glowing; and Nightjar, a male gadget vigilante in a dark grey armoured suit with a grappling line and a hood. Bright primary-coloured heroes against a dark city. No emblems that copy real heroes.
