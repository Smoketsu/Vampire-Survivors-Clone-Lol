Concept art sheet for a top-down Vampire Survivors-style game, in the exact pixel-art style of the two reference sprites (a hooded necromancer and a skeleton warrior): true pixel art, chunky readable pixels, a dark 1 px outline, a small limited palette, flat shading with a few tones, 3/4 top-down view, figures shown full body. The references are for style and scale only; do not copy them unless the scene asks for the necromancer or skeletons. Every female figure has an exaggerated hourglass figure and is fully clothed (chest and hips always covered). Male figures are clearly male. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI.

A lineup of the city's defenders against the undead, fully original designs with no real-world insignia, flags, agency names or police logos, standing in a row on a plain dark background, escalating left to right: 1) a male street thug with a baseball bat and a hoodie; 2) a male gang gunner with two pistols held sideways; 3) a male riot cop with a clear riot shield, helmet and baton; 4) a male SWAT breacher in black armour with a shotgun and a flashbang; 5) a female field medic in olive fatigues with a medical pack and a glowing green healing injector; 6) a male rifleman in modern combat gear; 7) a male machine gunner kneeling behind a tripod machine gun; 8) a small modern battle tank; 9) a small attack helicopter. Palette: asphalt grey, concrete, sodium orange, olive drab, hazard yellow, red and blue light accents.
