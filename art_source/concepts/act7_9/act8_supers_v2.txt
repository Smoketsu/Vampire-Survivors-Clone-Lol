Concept art sheet for a top-down Vampire Survivors-style game, in the exact pixel-art style of the two reference sprites (a hooded necromancer and a skeleton warrior): true pixel art, chunky readable pixels, a dark 1 px outline, a small limited palette, flat shading with a few tones, 3/4 top-down view, figures shown full body. The references are for style and scale only; do not copy them unless the scene asks for the necromancer or skeletons. Every female figure has an exaggerated hourglass figure and is fully clothed (chest and hips always covered). Male figures are clearly male. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI.

Five ORIGINAL superheroes posed together on a city rooftop at night. They must NOT resemble any existing comic book or film hero: no red speedster suits, no lightning-bolt emblems, no bat ears, no pointed cowls, no utility belts, no green-skinned giants, no chest emblems at all. In the centre Paragon, the Last Hero: a tall powerful male hero with silver hair, in white and pale gold layered armour plates, a short half-cape on one shoulder, floating, eyes glowing gold. Blitz: a lean male speedster in a teal and white racing suit with orange goggles pushed up on his bare head, a long white scarf, pale blue afterimages of himself trailing behind (no lightning). Atlas: a huge bald male strongman with a braided beard, in a bronze and burnt-orange wrestler's harness with chain bracers, normal skin tone, lifting a wrecked car overhead. Aurora: a female flying energy blaster in a sleek violet and teal suit with a long glowing teal scarf, hands glowing. Nightjar: a male gadget vigilante in a dark olive trench coat over light armour, a bird-like grey visor mask with a short beak shape, a grappling launcher on his wrist, no cape. Distinct, bright, readable silhouettes against a dark city.
