Concept art sheet for a top-down Vampire Survivors-style game, in the exact pixel-art style of the two reference sprites (a hooded necromancer and a skeleton warrior): true pixel art, chunky readable pixels, a dark 1 px outline, a small limited palette, flat shading with a few tones, 3/4 top-down view, figures shown full body. The references are for style and scale only; do not copy them unless the scene asks for the necromancer or skeletons. Every female figure has an exaggerated hourglass figure and is fully clothed (chest and hips always covered). Male figures are clearly male. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI.

A lineup of nine enemies of the Sanguine Court, a vampire faction, standing in a row on a plain dark background, each about the same height as the skeleton reference except the heavies: 1) a male thrall, a pale bitten village servant in rags with a vacant stare; 2) a male ghoul, hunched and grey, long claws, crouched as if eating; 3) a swarm of five small vampire bats; 4) a male blood knight in blood-red plate with a halberd; 5) a male chiropteran, a mutated bat-man with leathery wings spread and a screaming fanged face; 6) a werewolf mid-transformation: a male hunter with a silver crossbow on the left half, a huge grey wolf-beast on the right half; 7) a female crimson witch in a black and red gown with a pointed hat and a glowing red hex sigil in her hand; 8) a male bloated thrall, swollen with blood, large and round; 9) a female vampire countess in a black velvet gown with a deep neckline and a high slit, red eyes, a wisp of red mist at her hem. Palette: blood red, black velvet, candle gold, moonlit silver, pale skin.
