Concept art sheet for a top-down Vampire Survivors-style game, in the exact pixel-art style of the two reference sprites (a hooded necromancer and a skeleton warrior): true pixel art, chunky readable pixels, a dark 1 px outline, a small limited palette, flat shading with a few tones, 3/4 top-down view, figures shown full body. The references are for style and scale only; do not copy them unless the scene asks for the necromancer or skeletons. Every female figure has an exaggerated hourglass figure and is fully clothed (chest and hips always covered). Male figures are clearly male. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI.

A lineup of nine alien enemies from four alien races, standing in a row on a starfield-black background: insect Brood (violet chitin, acid green): 1) a small fast broodling, 2) a burrower with digging claws bursting from a dirt mound, 3) an acid spitter with a swollen glowing sac; grey Harvesters (pale grey skin, hull white): 4) a small grey alien drone with a probe tool, 5) a tall grey overseer floating with a psionic glow around its large head, 6) a small flying saucer casting a pale tractor beam; reptilian Vrask hunters (scaled, bone armour, amber): 7) a male reptilian hunter, half-shimmering with a cloaking effect, a shoulder plasma cannon; beings of living light, the Luminous (laser magenta and white): 8) a crystalline light sentinel firing a beam, 9) a female being of living light, the Star-Seer, in a flowing gown of light, holding a prism. Accents: plasma green, laser magenta, gunmetal.
