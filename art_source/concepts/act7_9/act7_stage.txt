Concept art sheet for a top-down Vampire Survivors-style game, in the exact pixel-art style of the two reference sprites (a hooded necromancer and a skeleton warrior): true pixel art, chunky readable pixels, a dark 1 px outline, a small limited palette, flat shading with a few tones, 3/4 top-down view, figures shown full body. The references are for style and scale only; do not copy them unless the scene asks for the necromancer or skeletons. Every female figure has an exaggerated hourglass figure and is fully clothed (chest and hips always covered). Male figures are clearly male. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI.

A gameplay mock-up seen from the top-down game camera: the cursed moorland village of Ravensmoor at night under a blood-red moon, crooked houses, a graveyard and a church. In the centre the hooded necromancer from the reference stands inside a ring of skeleton warriors (the Bone Ring). Blood knights in red plate press against the ring, and thin red threads of blood flow from the skeletons they hit back into the knights (they heal on hit). A swarm of bats flies over the ring toward the necromancer, their shadows on the ground. A werewolf is transforming at the edge. A sealed coffin with chains lies in the graveyard. Small figures at game scale, readable.
