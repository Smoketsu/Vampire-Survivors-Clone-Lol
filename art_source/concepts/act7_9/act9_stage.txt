Concept art sheet for a top-down Vampire Survivors-style game, in the exact pixel-art style of the two reference sprites (a hooded necromancer and a skeleton warrior): true pixel art, chunky readable pixels, a dark 1 px outline, a small limited palette, flat shading with a few tones, 3/4 top-down view, figures shown full body. The references are for style and scale only; do not copy them unless the scene asks for the necromancer or skeletons. Every female figure has an exaggerated hourglass figure and is fully clothed (chest and hips always covered). Male figures are clearly male. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI.

A gameplay mock-up seen from the top-down game camera: the crash site of a huge alien ship in the ruins of a city, a smoking crater, broken glass towers, purple alien eggs growing in corpses. In the centre the hooded necromancer from the reference stands inside a ring of skeleton warriors (the Bone Ring) with a glowing teal shield orb orbiting him. A laser-magenta beam sweeps across the ring and is reflected off the shield orb back toward a light-being sentinel. A small flying saucer above lifts one skeleton into the air in a pale tractor beam. Insect broodlings swarm in from the crater. Small figures at game scale, readable.
