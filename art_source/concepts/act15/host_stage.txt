Concept art sheet for a top-down Vampire Survivors-style game, in the exact pixel-art style of the two reference sprites (a hooded necromancer and a skeleton warrior): true pixel art, chunky readable pixels, a dark 1 px outline, a small limited palette, flat shading with a few tones, 3/4 top-down view, figures shown full body. The references are for style and scale only; do not copy them unless the scene asks for the necromancer or skeletons. Every female figure has an exaggerated hourglass figure and is fully clothed (chest and hips always covered). Male figures are clearly male. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI.

A gameplay mock-up seen from the top-down game camera: a stair of pale light rising through clouds from a ruined ashen ground, with floating silver rings. In the centre the hooded necromancer from the reference stands inside a ring of skeleton warriors (the Bone Ring). Shafts of white-blue light sweep across the ground and burn two skeletons; the shadow of a cloud pillar is a safe dark patch. Silver angels with mirror masks advance, each with a thin geometric halo ring around it; one angel's halo is cracking. A set of scales glows at the top edge. Small figures at game scale, readable. Palette: starlight white, pale silver, cerulean blue and dawn rose; NO crimson, NO red, no warm yellow gold; halos are thin geometric rings of white-blue light; the angels look otherworldly and strange, not like human knights or priests.
