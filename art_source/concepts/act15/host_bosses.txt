Concept art sheet for a top-down Vampire Survivors-style game, in the exact pixel-art style of the two reference sprites (a hooded necromancer and a skeleton warrior): true pixel art, chunky readable pixels, a dark 1 px outline, a small limited palette, flat shading with a few tones, 3/4 top-down view, figures shown full body. The references are for style and scale only; do not copy them unless the scene asks for the necromancer or skeletons. Every female figure has an exaggerated hourglass figure and is fully clothed (chest and hips always covered). Male figures are clearly male. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI.

The three bosses of heaven in one scene above the clouds: on the left Seraiel, Warden of the Gate, a towering male archangel in silver armour with four great wings and a flaming white sword before a vast pearl gate; in the middle the Throne, a colossal wheel within a wheel of silver and blue fire, its rims covered in eyes, rolling; on the right the Voice, a six-winged seraph of white light whose face is hidden behind two wings, two wings covering its feet and two spread, standing in an enormous white rose of light. Palette: starlight white, pale silver, cerulean blue and dawn rose; NO crimson, NO red, no warm yellow gold; halos are thin geometric rings of white-blue light; the angels look otherworldly and strange, not like human knights or priests.
