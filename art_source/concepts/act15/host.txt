Concept art sheet for a top-down Vampire Survivors-style game, in the exact pixel-art style of the two reference sprites (a hooded necromancer and a skeleton warrior): true pixel art, chunky readable pixels, a dark 1 px outline, a small limited palette, flat shading with a few tones, 3/4 top-down view, figures shown full body. The references are for style and scale only; do not copy them unless the scene asks for the necromancer or skeletons. Every female figure has an exaggerated hourglass figure and is fully clothed (chest and hips always covered). Male figures are clearly male. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI.

Key art for the Celestial Host, the armies of heaven: in the foreground a row of angels and holy beings: a male angelic legionary in white and gold armour with a spear, a female choir angel singing with light around her, a cherub-faced four-winged guardian, a burning wheel covered in eyes (an ophan), a male judge angel with a sword and scales. Behind them, enthroned in blinding white light on clouds, a colossal six-winged seraph with a hidden face and a flaming sword. Palette: white, pale gold, sky blue, rose dawn.
