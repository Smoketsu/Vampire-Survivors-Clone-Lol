Concept art sheet for a top-down Vampire Survivors-style game, in the exact pixel-art style of the two reference sprites (a hooded necromancer and a skeleton warrior): true pixel art, chunky readable pixels, a dark 1 px outline, a small limited palette, flat shading with a few tones, 3/4 top-down view, figures shown full body. The references are for style and scale only; do not copy them unless the scene asks for the necromancer or skeletons. Every female figure has an exaggerated hourglass figure and is fully clothed (chest and hips always covered). Male figures are clearly male. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI.

A lineup of the armies of heaven on a plain deep-blue background, in a row: 1) a swarm of small floating lesser lights, orbs of white light with tiny wings; 2) a male Virtue, a tall silver-armoured angel spearman with a featureless mirror mask and four wings; 3) a female choir angel in flowing pale-blue robes singing, sheet-like light around her; 4) a female Power, a warrior angel with a blade of white flame and silver armour; 5) a male Herald with a long silver trumpet and wings; 6) a male Dominion with a sceptre and a crown of light rings; 7) a Cherub as in old scripture: a four-faced winged creature (lion, ox, eagle and man) with four wings covered in eyes; 8) an Ophan, a burning silver wheel within a wheel covered in eyes. Palette: starlight white, pale silver, cerulean blue and dawn rose; NO crimson, NO red, no warm yellow gold; halos are thin geometric rings of white-blue light; the angels look otherworldly and strange, not like human knights or priests.
