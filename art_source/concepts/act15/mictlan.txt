Concept art sheet for a top-down Vampire Survivors-style game, in the exact pixel-art style of the two reference sprites (a hooded necromancer and a skeleton warrior): true pixel art, chunky readable pixels, a dark 1 px outline, a small limited palette, flat shading with a few tones, 3/4 top-down view, figures shown full body. The references are for style and scale only; do not copy them unless the scene asks for the necromancer or skeletons. Every female figure has an exaggerated hourglass figure and is fully clothed (chest and hips always covered). Male figures are clearly male. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI.

Key art for an Aztec-inspired underworld act, Mictlan: in the foreground a row of warriors and spirits: a male jaguar knight with an obsidian club, a male eagle knight, a female priestess with a feathered headdress and a jade mask, a skeletal xolo dog, a male warrior with a capture net, an obsidian-wind spirit. Behind them a stepped pyramid at night under a pale fifth sun, and enthroned on it the Lord and Lady of the Dead, a skeletal male god with a headdress of owl feathers and a skeletal female goddess with a skull face and marigold garlands. Palette: jade green, obsidian black, turquoise, gold, marigold orange, blood red accents.
