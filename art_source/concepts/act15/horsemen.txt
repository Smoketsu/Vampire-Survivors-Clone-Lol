Concept art sheet for a top-down Vampire Survivors-style game, in the exact pixel-art style of the two reference sprites (a hooded necromancer and a skeleton warrior): true pixel art, chunky readable pixels, a dark 1 px outline, a small limited palette, flat shading with a few tones, 3/4 top-down view, figures shown full body. The references are for style and scale only; do not copy them unless the scene asks for the necromancer or skeletons. Every female figure has an exaggerated hourglass figure and is fully clothed (chest and hips always covered). Male figures are clearly male. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI.

Key art for the Four Horsemen: in the foreground a row of their servants: a male plague-rider in a beaked mask, a gaunt female famine wraith with a set of scales, a male war-herald in red iron with a broken sword, a robed reaper with a scythe, swarming locusts and crows. Behind them, larger, the Four Horsemen on their horses: Pestilence on a white horse with a bow, War on a red horse with a great sword, Famine on a black horse holding scales, and in the centre, biggest, Death, a skeletal rider in a pale tattered cloak on a pale ghostly horse, holding an hourglass and a scythe. A grey sky with a black sun. Palette: bone white, ash grey, rust red, sickly green, pale gold.
