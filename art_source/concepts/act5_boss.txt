BOSS CONCEPT, two halves side by side in one wide picture. LEFT: SHOGUN KUROGANE on a raised wooden command platform inside a castle courtyard, an armoured warlord in black-and-gold o-yoroi with a huge crescent-moon crest on his kabuto, a war fan raised to signal; behind and around him, rank after rank of his army with tall war banners in regiments (red, black, white, gold banners), marching toward the viewer: his army is his health bar. RIGHT: AMATERASU, the sun goddess, floating above a shrine gate (torii) at the edge of the sky, a radiant woman in the figure-redesign style (exaggerated hourglass figure, fully clothed in flowing layered white, gold and crimson kimono robes), long black hair with a golden sun disc halo behind her head, a mirror in her hands sending a long blazing beam of golden light down across the ground; the sun's arc drawn faintly across the sky from the east (left of her) to the west. At the bottom middle, small but clear, the violet-hooded necromancer with his bone staff and violet flame and his skeletons. Colours: lacquer black #15131A, gold #E0A92E #FFDC66 #FFFAE8, crimson #C9352B, sakura #F7C9D5, sky dawn #F28C4A, necromancer violet.

STYLE: concept art for a dark 2D top-down pixel-art game (attached: the game's own sprites, the necromancer and his skeletons, for the pixel size, outline weight and look). True pixel art: crisp square pixels, every pixel a flat colour block about 4 image pixels wide, nearest-neighbour, a 1-pixel dark outline around every figure, light from the upper left, at most 3 shades per material, big bold readable shapes, faces that read clearly. No dithering, no gradients, no soft glow haze, no blur, no text, no letters, no labels, no watermark, no UI, no border. Fully opaque.
