BOSS CONCEPT: Mount Olympus at its summit, a wide scene. ZEUS, king of the gods, is a colossal figure towering over the marble temple ruins of the summit: a muscular bearded old god in a white-and-gold toga, a crown of gold laurel, eyes blazing white-blue, raising a huge jagged lightning bolt (#FFFAE8 core, #9ED0FF, #3D7FD0) in one hand, storm clouds churning behind him. Beside him, smaller, the other gods of the boss rush on golden thrones: ARES in black-bronze armour with a spear, ARTEMIS (figure-redesign style: hourglass figure, fully clothed in a short silver hunting chiton with a silver bow and a crescent moon tiara), POSEIDON with a trident and sea-green beard. At the bottom left, small but clear, the violet-hooded necromancer with his bone staff and violet flame and a phalanx of his skeleton soldiers climbing the marble steps. Lightning strikes leave telegraphed scorched circles on the steps. Colours: marble #D9D3C3 #F4F0E6, gold #E0A92E #FFDC66, storm #1A2240 #3B4A7A, lightning #9ED0FF #FFFAE8, bronze #B07A33, necromancer violet #4A2569 #7A42AD #A35CFA.

STYLE: concept art for a dark 2D top-down pixel-art game (attached: the game's own sprites, the necromancer and his skeletons, for the pixel size, outline weight and look). True pixel art: crisp square pixels, every pixel a flat colour block about 4 image pixels wide, nearest-neighbour, a 1-pixel dark outline around every figure, light from the upper left, at most 3 shades per material, big bold readable shapes, faces that read clearly. No dithering, no gradients, no soft glow haze, no blur, no text, no letters, no labels, no watermark, no UI, no border. Fully opaque.
