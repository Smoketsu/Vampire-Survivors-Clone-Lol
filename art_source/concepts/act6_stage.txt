STAGE MOOD (top-down three-quarter view like the attached game, as a gameplay mock-up without UI): THE BURNING GATE, stage 16. A plain of black ash and cracked obsidian, the river of fire (Phlegethon) along one side, chained cages and spikes, and glowing HELL PITS on the ground (round pools of bubbling lava with a bright rim, damaging anyone standing in them). Two DEMON PORTALS stand on the plain: tall ovals of swirling crimson fire framed by horned black stone arches, with imps and hell knights stepping out of them. In the middle the violet-hooded necromancer inside a ring of his skeleton army fights demons; a succubus at the ring's edge blows a pink charm kiss and one skeleton of the ring has pink hearts over it and is turning on its friends. A giant gate of hell in the background, shaped like a screaming horned face. Colours: ash #26272A #4A4446, obsidian #120A10, lava #D9381E #FF8C26 #FFDC66, portal crimson #A8245E #FF4A6E, charm pink #FF7BC8, necromancer violet.

STYLE: concept art for a dark 2D top-down pixel-art game (attached: the game's own sprites, the necromancer and his skeletons, for the pixel size, outline weight and look). True pixel art: crisp square pixels, every pixel a flat colour block about 4 image pixels wide, nearest-neighbour, a 1-pixel dark outline around every figure, light from the upper left, at most 3 shades per material, big bold readable shapes, faces that read clearly. No dithering, no gradients, no soft glow haze, no blur, no text, no letters, no labels, no watermark, no UI, no border. Fully opaque.
