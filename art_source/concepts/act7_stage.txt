STAGE MOOD (top-down three-quarter view like the attached game, as a gameplay mock-up without UI): RAVENSMOOR, stage 19. A cursed moorland village at night under a huge BLOOD-RED MOON: dark purple heather, crooked cottages, a ruined old church, bare black trees, mist, sealed stone coffins lying on the ground. In the middle the violet-hooded necromancer inside a ring of his skeleton army fights. BLOODFEAST: vampire knights and thralls bite the skeletons and thin red threads run from the skeletons to the vampires, who glow with a red blood shield. NIGHT WINGS: a swarm of black bats and two bat-folk fly over the ring, each with a dark swoop shadow on the ground where it will land. A female vampire noble in a velvet gown at the edge of the ring fires a red drain beam; a werewolf mid-transformation lunges in. Colours: night purple #1B1030 #3A2455, blood red #A8141E #E0303A, moon red #C0303A, candle gold #E0A92E, mist grey-blue #8FA3BF, necromancer violet.

STYLE: concept art for a dark 2D top-down pixel-art game (attached: the game's own sprites, the necromancer and his skeletons, for the pixel size, outline weight and look). True pixel art: crisp square pixels, every pixel a flat colour block about 4 image pixels wide, nearest-neighbour, a 1-pixel dark outline around every figure, light from the upper left, at most 3 shades per material, big bold readable shapes, faces that read clearly. No dithering, no gradients, no soft glow haze, no blur, no text, no letters, no labels, no watermark, no UI, no border. Fully opaque.
