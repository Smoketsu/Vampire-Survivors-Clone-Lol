A sprite sheet of 6 small FLOOR DECALS for a top-down 2D pixel-art game, on a fully TRANSPARENT background.
Layout: a grid of 3 columns x 2 rows; each decal sits alone in the centre of its own cell, with wide empty
transparent gaps between them (nothing touches, nothing overlaps a cell border).

Every decal is a flat detail LYING ON THE GROUND, seen STRAIGHT FROM ABOVE (orthographic top-down view, like a map:
no perspective, no 3/4 view, no side view, no visible height or thickness). NO cast shadow, no drop shadow, no
shading under it, no glow halo, no outline frame. Nothing may look like a standing object or an obstacle: everything
is flattened into the ground, like a stain, a scatter or a thing that fell over and lies flat.

LOW-RESOLUTION pixel art: each decal is only about 12 to 24 pixels across, shown enlarged so every pixel is a big crisp
square block (nearest-neighbour), with chunky 2-3 pixel details. No gradients, no dithering, no soft blur, no
anti-aliasing, no texture noise. Very DARK, LOW CONTRAST and QUIET colours, close to the dark floor they lie on
(the attached image is that floor: match its darkness and pixel size, but do NOT draw the floor itself, the
background must stay transparent).
