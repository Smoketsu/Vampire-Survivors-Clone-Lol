A sprite sheet of 6 small FLOOR DECALS for a top-down 2D pixel-art game, on a fully TRANSPARENT background.
Layout: a grid of 3 columns x 2 rows; each decal sits alone in the centre of its own cell, with wide empty
transparent gaps between them (nothing touches, nothing overlaps a cell border).

Every decal is a flat detail LYING ON THE GROUND, seen STRAIGHT FROM ABOVE (orthographic top-down view, like a map:
no perspective, no 3/4 view, no side view, no visible height or thickness). NO cast shadow, no drop shadow, no
shading under it, no glow halo, no outline frame. Nothing may look like a standing object or an obstacle: everything
is flattened into the ground, like a stain, a scatter or a thing that fell over and lies flat.

LOW-RESOLUTION pixel art: each decal is only about 16 to 22 pixels across, shown enlarged so every pixel is a big crisp
square block (nearest-neighbour), with chunky 2-3 pixel details. No gradients, no dithering, no soft blur, no
anti-aliasing, no texture noise. Very DARK, LOW CONTRAST and QUIET colours, close to the dark floor they lie on
(the attached image is that floor: match its darkness and pixel size, but do NOT draw the floor itself, the
background must stay transparent); the only bright pixels allowed are the tiny orange embers.

The decals, left to right, top to bottom:
1. A burnt-out campfire seen from straight above (about 18 x 16 pixels): a flat ring of small dark stones around black ash and charred sticks, two or three tiny orange embers in the middle.
2. A cold campfire ring (about 16 x 14 pixels): flat ring of dark stones, black ash, one charred log, one tiny orange ember.
3. A tent rope lying across the ground from a wooden tent peg driven into the soil (about 20 x 10 pixels): a thin dull-beige rope line, a small dark wooden peg head seen from above.
4. A loose coiled tent rope lying flat on the ground with a peg beside it (about 16 x 12 pixels).
5. A smashed wooden crate: its boards broken apart and lying flat, scattered on the ground (about 18 x 14 pixels), dark brown planks, a few bent nails.
6. Two loose crate boards and splinters lying flat on the ground (about 16 x 10 pixels).

Colours: ONLY flat fills from this list: #30261B dark dirt, #4F3320 dark brown (most), #8C6040 lighter brown, #1C110B near-black, #2A2724 ash grey, #7F785F dull beige (rope only), #FF8C26 orange (ONLY the tiny embers).
No text, no labels, no numbers, no border, no watermark.
