A sprite sheet of 6 small FLOOR DECALS for a top-down 2D pixel-art game, on a fully TRANSPARENT background.
Layout: a grid of 3 columns x 2 rows; each decal sits alone in the centre of its own cell, with wide empty
transparent gaps between them (nothing touches, nothing overlaps a cell border).

Every decal is a flat detail LYING ON THE GROUND, seen STRAIGHT FROM ABOVE (orthographic top-down view, like a map:
no perspective, no 3/4 view, no side view, no visible height or thickness). NO cast shadow, no drop shadow, no
shading under it, no glow halo, no outline frame. Nothing may look like a standing object or an obstacle: everything
is flattened into the ground, like a stain, a scatter or a thing that fell over and lies flat.

LOW-RESOLUTION pixel art: each decal is only about 10 to 24 pixels across, shown enlarged so every pixel is a big crisp
square block (nearest-neighbour), with chunky 2-3 pixel details. No gradients, no dithering, no soft blur, no
anti-aliasing, no texture noise. DARK, LOW CONTRAST and QUIET colours, close to the dark plank floor and dark paving
they lie on (the attached images are those floors: match their darkness and pixel size, but do NOT draw the floor
itself, the background must stay transparent). A drowned seaside chapel and a seawall promenade, at night.

The decals, left to right, top to bottom:
1. A crust of dark coral grown flat over the floor (about 20 x 15 pixels): an irregular lumpy patch of dusky mauve-purple coral with a few paler knobs.
2. A cluster of five or six barnacles stuck flat to the floor (about 18 x 8 pixels): small grey cones seen from above, each a ring with a dark centre hole.
3. The sigil of the Ancient Eye carved into the floor (about 24 x 24 pixels): a thin carved circle with an almond eye shape inside and a round pupil, a faint magenta stain in the pupil, otherwise dark carved grooves.
4. A rusted iron mooring ring lying flat on the stones (about 12 x 12 pixels): a round iron ring with a small iron plate.
5. A short length of rusted iron chain lying flat in a slight curve (about 26 x 7 pixels): four or five chunky oval links.
6. A single grey-white gull feather lying flat (about 11 x 5 pixels).

Colours: ONLY flat fills from this list: #0F141C very dark grey, #3B4352 dark grey, #6B7A94 grey, #A7B3C6 pale grey (feather, barnacle rims), #1C110B very dark brown, #4F3320 rust brown, #8C6040 light rust, #1E0A22 very dark purple, #5A2262 dark magenta-purple (coral), #B04CC0 magenta (only one or two pixels in the sigil's pupil), #06151A very dark teal, #1B4650 dark teal.
No text, no labels, no numbers, no border, no watermark.
